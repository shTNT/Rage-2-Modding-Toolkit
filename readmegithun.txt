RAGE 2 Modding Toolkit v1.1

Features:
- Extracts all 13 .arc files in ~30s (16 threads)
- Identifies 11,714 files by hash using community filelist
- Converts .avtx textures to editable .dds
- Organizes content by type (textures, audio, video, UI, data)
- Ready for Stable Diffusion / Chainner upscaling pipelines
- Auto-copies oo2core_7_win64.dll from your game install

Requirements:
- Windows 10/11
- RAGE 2 (Steam or Epic)
- ~25 GB free disk space

Usage: unzip, double-click RAGE2-TOOLKIT.bat, follow the menu.

Credits:
- PredatorCZ (ApexToolset)
- Microsoft (DirectXTex / texconv)
- DECA community (filelist)
- RAGE 2 modding community

NOT redistributable: oo2core_7_win64.dll (RAD Game Tools)
The toolkit copies it automatically from your game install.
